## 2. Tailored Fashion Typography System

- **Editorial Headings & Campaign Display**:
  - `Syne` (Weights: 700, 800) — Distinctive, bold, architectural sans-serif created specifically for high-end luxury fashion branding and lookbooks.
  - `Playfair Display` & `DM Serif Display` — Refined, high-contrast serif accents for editorial romance and magazine titles.
- **Body & Navigation Links**:
  - `Plus Jakarta Sans` & `Manrope` (Weights: 400, 500, 600) — Geometric clean sans-serif optimized for crisp screen rendering and effortless scanning.
- **Camera Metadata & GSM Specifications**:
  - `Space Grotesk` & `Space Mono` — Technical monospace for camera viewfinders (`REC [●] RAW`, coordinates `28.6139° N, 77.2090° E`, GSM labels).
